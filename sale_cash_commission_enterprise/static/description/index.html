Advanced Cash-Based Sales Commission (Enterprise)

The most accurate way to automate commissions based on actual payments.

Manage sales commissions with absolute precision. Our module handles partial payments, credit notes, and multi-employee assignments seamlessly within Odoo Enterprise.

Why Enterprise Teams Choose This Module

  • Cash-Basis Precision: Commissions updated via real-time payment reconciliation.
  • Partial Reconciliation: Automatically handles partial payments & splits.
  • Credit Note Support: Automatic deduction of commissions on returned invoices.
  • Multi-Employee Assignment: Assign commissions directly on the Invoice.
  • Smart Validation: Built-in safety checks for financial integrity.
  • Seamless Payroll Integration: Automated push to Odoo Payslips.

Complete Commission Workflow

1. Define Rules: Set up your commission logic within standard Salary Rules.

2. Custom Rates: Define specific commission percentages per employee/product.

3. Direct Assignment: Easily select commission beneficiaries directly from the Invoice form.

4. Calculated Lines: Track every detail, including partial payments and credit note adjustments.

5. Period Management: Manage and close commission periods professionally.

6. Smart Validation: Built-in protections preventing errors before payroll generation.

7. Automated Payout: Final commission amounts appear instantly on the Employee Payslip.
